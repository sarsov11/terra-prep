Question 4 of 10Law as of 2026-09-29

Harbor Fabrication, a calendar-year manufacturer, bought a new press in 2026. The invoice price was $60,000. Harbor also paid $3,900 of sales tax, $1,800 of freight to deliver the press, and $2,300 to have the press installed and tested. After installation, Harbor spent $1,000 to train its employees to run the press. What is Harbor's cost basis in the press?

  1. A$60,000
  2. B$65,700
  3. C$68,000 Correct
  4. D$69,000 Your answer

Notes on each choice

A
Not chosen · WrongOnly the invoice price. Sales tax, freight and installation are costs of acquiring and readying the asset and belong in basis.
B
Not chosen · WrongIncludes tax and freight but omits the $2,300 installation and testing cost, which is also capitalized into basis.
C
CorrectBasis of purchased property is its cost, including sales tax, freight, and the installation and testing costs needed to place it in service: $60,000 + $3,900 + $1,800 + $2,300 = $68,000. Employee training is an operating expense, not part of the asset's cost.
  1. Invoice price 60,000
  2. Add sales tax 3,900, freight 1,800, installation 2,300
  3. 60,000 + 3,900 + 1,800 + 2,300 = 68,000
  4. Training (1,000) is expensed, not capitalized
D
Your answer · WrongCapitalizes the $1,000 of employee training as well. Training occurs after the asset is ready for use and is a currently deductible operating cost.

Source: IRC §1012(a); Reg §1.1012-1(a); Pub 551 · Confidence: Certain · Logged as misconception
