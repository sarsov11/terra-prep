Question Application

Harbor Fabrication, a calendar-year manufacturer, bought a new press in 2026. The invoice price was $60,000. Harbor also paid $3,900 of sales tax, $1,800 of freight to deliver the press, and $2,300 to have the press installed and tested. After installation, Harbor spent $1,000 to train its employees to run the press. What is Harbor's cost basis in the press?

A$60,000
B$65,700
C$68,000Correct
D$69,000Your answer

IRC §1012(a); Reg §1.1012-1(a); Pub 551 · Confidence: Certain

Notes by choice

AOnly the invoice price. Sales tax, freight and installation are costs of acquiring and readying the asset and belong in basis.
BIncludes tax and freight but omits the $2,300 installation and testing cost, which is also capitalized into basis.
CBasis of purchased property is its cost, including sales tax, freight, and the installation and testing costs needed to place it in service. Employee training is an operating expense, not part of the asset's cost.
60,000 + 3,900 + 1,800 + 2,300 = 68,000
DCapitalizes the $1,000 of employee training as well. Training occurs after the asset is ready for use and is a currently deductible operating cost.
