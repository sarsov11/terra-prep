Harbor Fabrication, a calendar-year manufacturer, bought a new press in 2026. The invoice price was $60,000. Harbor also paid $3,900 of sales tax, $1,800 of freight to deliver the press, and $2,300 to have the press installed and tested. After installation, Harbor spent $1,000 to train its employees to run the press. What is Harbor's cost basis in the press?

D · Wrong

Capitalizes the $1,000 of employee training as well. Training occurs after the asset is ready for use and is a currently deductible operating cost.

C · Correct

Basis of purchased property is its cost, including sales tax, freight, and the installation and testing costs needed to place it in service: $60,000 + $3,900 + $1,800 + $2,300 = $68,000. Employee training is an operating expense, not part of the asset's cost.
A · WrongOnly the invoice price. Sales tax, freight and installation are costs of acquiring and readying the asset and belong in basis.
B · WrongIncludes tax and freight but omits the $2,300 installation and testing cost, which is also capitalized into basis.

IRC §1012(a); Reg §1.1012-1(a); Pub 551 · Law as of 2026-09-29
